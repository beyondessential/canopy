C. Alternative: edit a scope as text

The pencil on a scope, or Ctrl+Shift+E while focus is anywhere in that scope, swaps its rows for an editor: one name: value per line, YAML values. Secrets read !secret and are left alone unless retyped. Ctrl+Enter saves the difference as sets and removes; Esc discards.
